02 — WHY WE'RE BUILDING IT

Big TV bills shouldn't be the price of a better experience.

CORDKIN TV began with a simple idea: families should have more control over how they watch, without losing the convenience they loved from traditional TV and DVR services.

We're developing a thoughtful player with planned live viewing, recording, scheduling and playback capabilities for compatible, authorized sources.

03 — YOUR CONTENT, YOUR CHOICE

Your source.
Your responsibility.

CORDKIN TV is a software player and cloud DVR service, not a television channel or programming subscription. You connect a compatible source that you are authorized to access.

Before recording, you must confirm that your provider's terms and applicable law permit it. A valid subscription or a playable stream does not necessarily include recording rights. User-requested recordings are for authorized account use; the original programs remain the property of their respective rights holders.

CORDKIN TV cannot guarantee a third-party provider's licensing, reliability, availability, or recording permissions. We still comply with legal obligations applicable to our own service.
